# salaries-EDA
Overview
This project performs Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on employee salary data to uncover insights such as salary distribution, trends, and correlations with other employee attributes like department, education level, and years of experience.

Objective
Analyze and visualize salary distributions.

Identify factors that influence employee salaries.

Gain insights into salary trends across departments and education levels.

Dataset
The dataset contains the following columns:

Employee ID: Unique identifier for each employee.

Department: The department the employee works in (e.g., HR, Finance, IT).

Education Level: The highest education level of the employee (e.g., Bachelor's, Master's, PhD).

Years of Experience: The total years of experience the employee has.

Salary: The annual salary of the employee.

Key Insights
Visualizations of salary distributions by department and education level.

Correlation analysis between salary and years of experience.

Identification of outliers and trends in the salary data.

Technologies Used
Python: The primary programming language.

Pandas: For data manipulation and analysis.

Matplotlib/Seaborn: For data visualization.
